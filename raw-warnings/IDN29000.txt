Warning to Sheep Graziers
for the Northern Tablelands forecast district.

Issued at 10:25 pm EDT on Saturday 28 November 2026.

Sheep graziers are warned that, rain and showers and northwesterly winds are
expected during early Sunday. Areas likely to be affected include parts of the
Northern Tablelands forecast district. There is a risk of losses of lambs and
sheep exposed to these conditions.

The next warning will be issued by 5:00 am EDT Sunday.
